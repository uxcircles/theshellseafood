Our story

Seaside France, in WC1

The Shell was founded by a group of seafood enthusiasts inspired by the relaxed elegance of seaside France, where outstanding seafood is meant to be shared, savoured and enjoyed without pretension.

“A truly great shellfish restaurant should be welcoming, indulgent and accessible.”

We work closely with trusted fishermen, producers and coastal suppliers. Seafood comes in fresh every day, and our chefs prepare each platter with care, so every dish reflects both quality and the season.

Good to know

Before you visit

  • Halal meat

    All of our meat is 100% halal, including every steak and the lamb chops.

  • Alcohol-free on request

    Some seafood is cooked with white wine. Tell us when you order and we’ll make it without.

  • Kids & vegans

    Beef or chicken burgers for children, and a truffle vegan linguine.

  • Allergies

    Our kitchen handles all major allergens. Please speak to your server before ordering.
